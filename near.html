Your ZIP

Closer than a state. Still a city average.

Type a ZIP code and we open the city page whose Census area actually contains it. The dollar figures stay the city average, or a named-area example we can cite. We do not publish a price for the ZIP itself.

Check a ZIP

Five digits. The lookup runs in your browser after this page loads a Census crosswalk. Submitting puts the ZIP in the page address so you can share the result. We do not keep an account for it.

What a match means

The geography is the U.S. Census Bureau’s 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Area (ZCTA) to Place relationship file, plus 2020 Gazetteer internal points for distance. A ZCTA approximates a USPS ZIP code. It is not a USPS product. PO Box and unique ZIP codes are often absent, and a ZCTA boundary can disagree with the postal city people expect.

A city match means about half or more of that area’s land sits inside a Census place we already publish. You then get that city’s advertised 10x10 rate and six-month true average — the same numbers as on the city page. Partial overlap is labeled with the land share. We do not stretch a far-away city’s rate to fill a gap.

Miles are straight-line between Census internal points, not drive time. Other published cities within 60 miles are listed so you can compare city averages when a facility there is realistic. A lower average on that list is not a price at your ZIP.

Neighborhood examples

Where StorageCafe has published named areas and street rates, the result shows those examples with the date and the unit size the sentence actually names. Some lists are standard units, some are 10x10s, and some sentences never name the size. We do not relabel them as a central, suburban, and outer ring, and we do not assign your ZIP to one of the names. Cities without that list say so.

Limitation. This page will not get more precise than the sources. A facility on your block can sit far from the city average, and far from a neighborhood example a few miles away. Ask the facility for the month-four rate before you compare.
