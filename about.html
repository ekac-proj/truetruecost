About

A calmer way to compare storage

TrueTrueCost is a small, independent guide to the storage true cost — what a unit costs once the promo is over. It exists because the most common complaint about self-storage is the same everywhere: the advertised price is a teaser, and the rent goes up after you move in.

What TrueTrueCost is

TrueTrueCost is a product for true-cost storage pricing after promos. Public price data is researched, run through one transparent model, and published on simple pages.

What we publish

One page per city with typical advertised rates, rates after the first increase, and a six-month true cost for common unit sizes — plus a checklist and a calculator for testing a real quote. Read the methodology. Choosing a size first? The size guide maps a studio, apartment, or a car to 5x5–10x20.

What we don’t do

  • No live quotes, no “only 2 units left”, no countdown timers.
  • No paid placement. Partners can’t buy a better number.
  • We don’t collect your contact details.

Affiliate disclosure

Some “compare live quotes” buttons are, or will become, affiliate or referral links, marked rel="sponsored". If you book through one we may earn a commission at no cost to you. It never changes the estimates we publish.

Corrections

Prices move constantly. If a number looks wrong for your city, a correction contact will be listed here once the site is live.
