Privacy Policy

TrueTrueCost is a static guide to storage prices. This page explains the little data involved in reading it, and what can happen when you click a partner link.

Effective October 29, 2026. The site operator is TrueTrueCost.

What this site is

TrueTrueCost publishes estimated storage costs. There is no account, no checkout, and no form that asks for your name, email, or phone number. We don’t collect your contact details to use the pages.

What we collect

TrueTrueCost does not run its own analytics or advertising cookies. The six-month calculator and the size quiz run in your browser. Numbers and answers you enter stay on your device and are not sent to us.

The host that serves these pages may keep ordinary technical logs — such as an IP address, browser type, and the page requested — to operate and secure the site. We do not use those logs to build a profile of you.

Affiliate links and partner cookies

Some “compare” buttons are, or will become, affiliate or referral links. Those links are marked rel="sponsored". If you click one, you leave TrueTrueCost. The partner may set its own cookies or record the click so a commission can be attributed. We do not receive your name, and we do not control the partner’s cookies. A commission never changes the estimates we publish. See the affiliate disclosure.

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not keep a customer list to rent or trade.

California residents

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, gives you the right to know, delete, and correct personal information a business holds about you, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of that information. TrueTrueCost does not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in that law. Because we do not keep a file of visitors, there is typically nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. If you believe we hold personal information about you, contact via the site and we will respond.

Children

TrueTrueCost is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact via the site. See the About page. See also the Terms of Use.
