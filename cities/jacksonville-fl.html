What a storage unit in Jacksonville really costs

Jacksonville listings advertise non-climate 10x10 units from about $49, while typical street rates run about $107 in the city and about $116 across the metro. Asking rents are down sharply year over year as new facilities open, which helps a negotiation — and still is not the six-month cost after the promo ends.

How to avoid the promo trap

  1. Ask how long the rate is locked. "When is the earliest my rent can go up, and by how much was the last increase here?" Get it in writing.
  2. Ask for the full move-in total. Admin fee, required protection, lock, deposit, tax. A "$1 first month" can still cost $40–$80 to move in.
  3. Compare the month-4 price, not month 1. Run each quote through the calculator.
  4. Get two or three competing quotes for the same size and climate control, including at least one independent facility.
  5. Use your own insurance if allowed. Many renters and homeowners policies cover off-site storage; some facilities waive their plan with proof.
  6. Negotiate when the increase notice arrives. You usually get 30 days. Quote the current new-customer rate for your size and ask them to match it.
  7. Be ready to move or downsize. If the gap to the new-customer rate is large, a promo elsewhere can pay for a truck within two or three months.
  8. Prepay only if the rate is locked. Prepay discounts mean little if rent can still rise mid-term.
  9. Set a reminder for month three. That is when increase notices commonly start to arrive.

Notes for Jacksonville

  • StorageCafe lists Woodland Acres (~$87), Northbank (~$88) and Cedar Hills Estates (~$94) among the cheapest areas, while Spring Glen (~$355) and Southpoint (~$282) run highest.
  • Jacksonville's all-unit average is lowest around December (~$155) and highest in October (~$159), StorageCafe. The seasonal gap is small next to the year-over-year drop in asking rents.
  • About 726,000 sq ft of new storage is due in 2026, up 36% from last year, across 10 facilities (StorageCafe). The metro has roughly 10.1 sq ft per person. New buildings often advertise a low move-in rate — ask for the month-5 price before you reserve.
  • The metro's non-climate 10x10 asking rent is down 14.1% from a year ago (Storage Stats). Quote that when an increase notice arrives.
  • Extra Space's published admin fee is $50 on StorageUnitCosts city pages, higher than our $29 default; adjust the calculator if your facility charges more.
