Storage true cost

The price you see is not the price you pay.

Storage is sold on the first month — “$1 move-in”, “50% off”. What you actually pay is decided later, by fees at the counter and a rent increase a few months after your things are inside. We show the real six-month cost, city by city.

Choose a city What size do I need? Check a ZIP How it works

A ZIP opens a city we already publish. The rates stay the city average. We do not invent a ZIP price.

How it works

  1. Start with the real rate

    We average the typical street rate for each unit size from two or three public price sources — not the promo.

  2. Add what comes later

    A half-price first month, then the full rate, then a typical 30% existing-customer increase from month five.

  3. Include the fees

    An admin fee and a monthly protection plan. Divide by six: that is the true monthly cost.
